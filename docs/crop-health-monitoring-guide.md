# Crop Health Monitoring Guide

## Purpose

Crop health monitoring helps farmers record disease observations and
review changes in crop condition over time.

## Recording Observations

Record the following information for each observation:

- Crop name
- Suspected disease or symptom
- Approximate affected area percentage
- Observation date
- Additional notes

## Application Severity Categories

| Affected area | Application category |
|---|---|
| Below 10% | Low |
| 10% to below 30% | Moderate |
| 30% to 100% | High |

These categories are basic software indicators. They do not establish
a diagnosis or determine the treatment required.

## Monitoring Recommendations

1. Inspect crops regularly.
2. Record the date and visible symptoms.
3. Compare observations over time.
4. Keep notes about weather and recent farm activities.
5. Consult an agricultural expert if symptoms spread or worsen.

## Crop Rotation

The crop rotation utility identifies immediately repeated crop names
in a proposed sequence. This is only a basic check: effective rotation
planning must also account for crop families, pests, soil conditions,
local climate and regional agricultural recommendations.
